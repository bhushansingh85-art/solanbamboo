About Solan Bamboo Furniture

Welcome to Solan Bamboo Furniture, your ultimate destination for sustainable, handcrafted, and durable bamboo living solutions. Located in the pristine hills of Himachal Pradesh (Village Dharja, Oachghat, Solan), we take pride in blending traditional artisan craftsmanship with modern, eco-friendly designs.

Every piece of furniture we create—from our luxury lounge sets to classic round coffee tables and easy chairs—is meticulously built to bring natural elegance, timeless style, and unmatched comfort into your home and outdoor spaces. Thank you for supporting sustainable living!
